# Design System: CEO MANUU Portfolio

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Cinematic Empire"**

The visual direction is "Dark Luxury." It combines high-confidence imagery with a restrained but powerful color palette (Electric Blue and Soft Pink accents). The goal is to create an internationally credible, premium environment that feels like a boutique holding company rather than a generic template.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Dark Base**: Deep navy or black backgrounds for luxury and authority.
- **Selective Motion**: Minimal, high-quality motion (soft fade-ins, smooth scroll).
- **Generous Space**: Enough negative space to feel expensive.
- **Empire Grid**: A structured but rhythmic presentation of multi-industry ventures.

## 2. Colors

### Primary: Electric Blue
- **Value**: #0044FF
- **Usage**: Buttons, links, and high-value highlights. Must feel sharp, not "neon-cheap."

### Secondary: Soft Pink
- **Value**: #FF66B2
- **Usage**: Secondary buttons, labels, and subtle glows. Used with restraint to add personality without noise.

### Neutral
- **Midnight Obsidian** (#050510): The major background color for headers, sections, and footers.
- **Pure White / Light Gray**: For main content contrast on dark sections.

## 3. Typography

**Display Font**: Bodoni Moda (Italian-style serif)
**Body Font**: Jost (Humanist sans)

### Hierarchy
- **Display**: For large, confident hero headlines.
- **Body**: For concise paragraphs and descriptions.

## 4. Elevation

The system uses "Subtle Depth."
- **Cards**: Rounded cards with subtle shadows and blue/pink glow accents on hover.
- **Panels**: Clean rectangular panels for structure.

## 5. Components

### Hero Section
- Full-screen visual with founder imagery.
- Main Headline: "Building Africa's Next Business Empire."
- Two CTAs: "Explore Ventures" (Primary) and "Partner With Me" (Secondary).

### Ventures Grid
- 9-card section (Automotive, Healthcare, Lifestyle, Real Estate, Imports, Beauty & Wellness, Transport & Logistics, Digital Systems, Seroxide).
- Each card: Icon + Title + One-line descriptor.
- Hover state: Soft blue/pink glows.

### Seroxide Feature
- Split layout: Strong image/video on one side, brand statement on the other.

### Footer
- Minimal and elegant. Short signature line.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use high-quality founder imagery with dark overlays.
- **Do** maintain name consistency (CEO MANUU).
- **Do** use `clamp()` for fluid typography.
- **Do** ensure mobile versions feel deliberate and not collapsed.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use dense walls of text.
- **Don't** use random hype or slang.
- **Don't** over-decorate. Spacing and hierarchy come first.
- **Don't** make ventures look like unrelated side businesses.
