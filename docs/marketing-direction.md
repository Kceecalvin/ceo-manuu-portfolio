# CEO Manuu: marketing direction

## The role of the site

Make the founder easy to understand, make the offerings visible, and give each visitor a useful next step. The site should connect the businesses without presenting plans as already operating businesses.

## Product first

For every offering, aim for this sequence:

1. Show the real product, service or work.
2. Explain who it helps and why it matters.
3. Give one primary next step.

SeroTix now demonstrates this with actual desktop and mobile screens for discovery, Find My Vibe and the squad shortlist. Visitors can inspect the product and then try the real website.

For the other businesses, collect actual photographs, service examples and approved customer stories. The current brand graphics represent each business area; they are not evidence of a physical location or completed work. Yadis is excluded.

## Three useful paths

- Customers: go to the relevant product or send a scoped service inquiry.
- Collaborators and business partners: choose a business area and explain the opportunity.
- Press and media: understand the founder's story and send a media inquiry.

These inquiry categories are represented in the contact form. A separate “Build with Manuu” landing page could become useful once concrete offers and proof are available.

## Income opportunities to validate

- SeroTix: ticket sales and organizer inquiries. Show the discovery experience, then the specific event or organizer offer. Any commissions or paid promotion packages need explicit pricing and terms.
- Digital work: sell clearly scoped work backed by the SeroTix product—for example, a landing page, an event website or a mobile usability improvement. Scope, price and availability should be agreed before listing an offer.
- Operating service businesses: connect a real service menu to a booking or quote request. Do not add booking promises before availability and operations are confirmed.

Track qualified inquiries and completed sales, not just visits. No revenue is guaranteed by the portfolio itself.

## Next useful content

1. A real founder portrait and a short personal introduction.
2. A 20–30 second SeroTix walkthrough showing discovery, the vibe quiz and the next action.
3. One clear offer and proof example for each operating business.
4. Clear labels separating operating businesses, work in progress and future ideas.
5. Short founder updates about actual progress, rather than unsupported success claims.

Before adding elaborate games or more animation, test whether visitors can explain the offering and reach the right inquiry or product in a few clicks.
